Q&A
WHAT THE PUBLIC RECORD DOES — AND DOES NOT — SAY
Is this live-money performance?

No. This is a prospective paper-validation record for the frozen long-book system. It should not be described as live brokerage performance.

Does this prove alpha?

No. The forward record is nontrivial and may be consistent with useful predictive structure, but the sample is still limited. More independent forward evidence is required before making a strong alpha claim.

Why are tickers, signals and entry/exit details hidden?

The public Observatory is an evidence layer, not a strategy disclosure. Pattern identities, signals, rankings, qualification logic, precise fills and research internals are intentionally withheld so outcomes can be inspected without exposing enough information to reconstruct the engine.

What does “Current Capacity Estimate” mean?

It is an availability-adjusted planning estimate derived from the frozen capacity audit. It is not live-measured AUM, and the $100K figure is explicitly a projection rather than demonstrated deployable capital.

What does Challenge Argus actually test?

It asks how much execution friction the published cumulative result can absorb. You can impose slippage, spread/impact, timing penalties and brokerage costs. The calculation runs only in your browser and never changes the official record.

Why not reconstruct “real” slippage here?

Doing that honestly would require information the public feed intentionally withholds, including precise timing and execution details. The challenge tool exposes its assumptions instead of manufacturing false precision.

Can anything on this page affect tomorrow’s trades?

No. The public site has no trading authority and no write path into Argus. It consumes a sanitized post-close snapshot after the fact.
